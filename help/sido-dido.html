SIDO / DIDO

One blue button shows the mode you're in. Tap it to switch. It's remembered on your device.

SIDO

Single In, Double Out. Scoring starts right away. This is the default.

DIDO

Double In, Double Out. Scoring starts only after you hit a Double. Until then the suggestions just say Double In: that's your choice, so there's no target.

Using DIDO

  • Enter 0 for darts that don't count, and your Double's score when you hit it (40 for D20, 50 for the Bull). Anything that can't be a Double is turned down.
  • Undo can take back the double-in dart.
  • In Full Turn mode, the first turn that scores counts as your double in (enter 0 for turns that didn't double in). It has to be a Double or the Bull plus up to two more darts, so at most 170 (Bull/T20/T20).

Example

Starting a game of 301 (DIDO). Your first dart is a single 20: enter 0. Your second is D18: enter 36. The app now subtracts from 301 and you're on 265. Until that second dart, nothing was taken off.
