Hidden Routes

Hide any suggested checkout or set up route you don't want, and the rest still show. It works with Custom Routes or on its own.

From Settings

  1. Tap , then Hidden Routes, then Hide routes.
  2. Enter a score. Its Checkouts routes and Set up rows appear as a checklist, hidden ones already checked.
  3. Check what you don't want and tap Save ("Saved. 122 hides 2 routes.").

To show a route again for good, tap the eye next to it in the list.

From the main screen

Press and hold any route (half a second; right-click on a computer) and tap Hide this route. To show it again for good, press and hold it and tap Show this route again. This is also how you hide a route that only shows with 1 or 2 darts in hand.

Looking at what's hidden

Tap Show hidden routes to see hidden routes in amber for this turn only. They stay hidden, and go away again with the next dart or score.

What can't be hidden

  • A 1-dart finish.
  • A custom route (remove it from Custom Routes first).
  • The only route there is.

If you hide every Set up row, only Checkouts shows.

Hidden vs. Doubles to Avoid. Doubles to Avoid hides every route that ends on a Double you dislike, across all scores. Hidden Routes hides one specific route on one score, e.g. a T19 you never hit on 122.
