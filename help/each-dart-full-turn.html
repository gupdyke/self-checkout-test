Each Dart / Full Turn

One blue button shows the mode you're in. Tap it to switch. It's remembered on your device.

Each Dart

Enter each dart on its own. The routes update after every dart, so you always see your best next throw.

  • Enter 0 for a dart that didn't score. Without it, Session Stats can't count your darts correctly.
  • On your last dart of a turn, a Treble that might leave a bogey number is flagged (see Set up). Full Turn mode never knows which dart you're on, so it can't.

Full Turn

Enter the combined score of all three darts, e.g. 140, or 0 for a turn that scored nothing. The routes update once per turn.

  • A total three darts can't make (like 179) is turned down.
  • Busts are caught for you: going below zero, leaving 1, or "checking out" a score no turn can finish.
  • On a checkout, tap Darts Used so your stats are correct.
  • Switching modes in the middle of a turn keeps the darts already entered; the next entry starts a new turn.

Which one?

Playing a real game, scoring as you go: Each Dart. You get the most from the app when it sees every dart: after a T20 on 122 it recomputes your 2-dart out right away.

Someone else keeps score, or you only want the total: Full Turn. Enter the total score for the round and the app does the rest.

Example

Starting from 501, you throw T20/T20/T20. In Each Dart mode you enter 60, 60, 60 and the routes update after each one. In Full Turn mode you enter 180 once. Either way you're left on 321.
