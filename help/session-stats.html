Session Stats and Darts Used

Session Stats

Tap (top left, next to Settings ) to see the current game, or the one you just finished: each round's darts, what it scored, what was left, and the total darts thrown.

  • In Full Turn mode, a round shows how many darts the turn took instead of each dart.
  • Busts and the checkout are marked. A bust in Full Turn mode shows the total you entered crossed out, and a bust counts as 3 darts whichever dart it came on.
  • It starts over when you enter a new score.

Darts Used

When you check out in Full Turn mode you're asked how many darts it took: tap 1, 2 or 3 (only the counts that are possible for that score show). The keypad says "Select number of darts used" until you do.

Full Turn mode only knows the round's total, not how many darts it took. Without your answer, a checkout counts as 3 darts and your stats look worse than they were.

Example: you're on 40 and hit D20 with your first dart. Tap 1. Your session shows a 1-dart checkout instead of 3.

Saving a Session

Tap Save session at the bottom of Session Stats to keep it on your device. The button says Saved ✓ until the session changes.

Your saved sessions are in Settings under Saved Sessions, newest first. Each shows the starting score, the number of rounds and darts, the date, and Out if you checked out. Tap one to see its rounds.

  • Delete this session removes the one you have open.
  • Clear all removes every saved session, after asking.

Saved sessions stay on this device only. Clearing your browser data, or uninstalling the app, deletes them, unless you exported them first. To keep your sessions or move them to a new device, use Import / Export in Settings.
