Doubles to Avoid

Don't like a Double? Open Settings and check it under Doubles to Avoid. The suggested routes will leave out any that finish on a checked Double, unless the alternative would need an extra dart.

Examples

100, 3 darts, D20 checkedCheckouts60, 40 (T20, D20)

Still T20/D20. The only other way out costs a dart (T20/S4/D18), and avoiding never costs a dart.

95, D19 checkedCheckouts57, 38 (T19, D19)

A finish on a checked Double is never hidden when there's no other way out.

81, 2 darts, D12 checkedCheckouts45, 36 (T15, D18)

T19/D12 is left out; T15/D18 takes its place.

Show hidden routes

When a checked Double hides a route, Show hidden routes appears under the list. Tap it to see the hidden routes in amber, and tap Hide hidden routes to hide them again. They return for this turn only.

To avoid seeing this link on the main screen, uncheck Warn me when routes are hidden in Settings.

Good to know

  • The Bull isn't on the list.
  • Doubles to Avoid never hides a custom route.
  • A 1-dart finish always shows.
  • If avoiding hides every route with the fewest darts, it shows up to three routes to other Doubles with the same number of darts. If there's no other way out, a checkout is never hidden.
  • To hide one route instead of a whole Double, see Hidden Routes.
