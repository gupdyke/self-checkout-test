Set up

Where to aim to set up your next turn, when you and/or your opponent aren't on a makeable finish.

When it appears

  • Beneath Checkouts on the scores where finishing and setting up have different options.
  • Alone, in blue and underlined, when there's no checkout with the darts you have left (but there would be with more). Tap it to see "No checkout with 1 dart" (or 2).
  • Alone, not underlined, when there's no checkout this turn: above 170 and on bogey numbers. There's nothing to tap, since your darts in hand can't get you to a finish.

You can't finish 90 with one dart, so you're not choosing a checkout, you're choosing what to leave. T18 leaves 36 and S18 leaves 72 while T20 leaves 30 and S20 leaves 70. Your choice.

T20 → D15 / S20 → 70 Tap Set up: "No checkout with 1 dart"

Above 170, and bogey numbers

You can't check out from there, so the box shows what T20, T19 and T18 (and their Singles) leave.

When T20 isn't the best Treble to start on, the better one is highlighted in blue. Aim for it: it's most likely to leave a finish at the end of your turn and keep you off a bogey number. Tap a blue or amber row to see its explanation.

Amber rows (last dart, Each Dart mode)

Above 170 or on a bogey number, with one dart in hand, a row turns amber if its Treble or Single would leave you with no finish when presented alongside a row that would lead to a finish. It's still listed, so you know to avoid it.

Only the rows to avoid are amber. On 177, 1 dart, T20 and T19 are plain and T18 is amber (S18 leaves 159, a bogey number).

177, 1 dartT20 → 117 / S20 → 157
T19 → 120 / S19 → 158
T18 → 123 / S18 → 159 Tap the amber row: "S18 leaves a bogey"

When nothing is highlighted

If no row leaves a finish, there's nothing to steer toward or away from, so no row is colored: on 501 you just see T20, T19 and T18.

The Bull first

On your last dart with 121, 122, 123 or 125, the Bull is listed first, in blue. The Bull and the 25 both leave a Treble to Double, which no Treble or its Single can do.

Hide it, or save it

Press and hold a Set up row to hide it in Hidden Routes or to add it to Custom Routes. Above 170 and on bogey numbers, Set up rows can't be hidden or added to Custom Routes. When Set up is visible beneath Checkouts, a Custom Route shows alone with a ★ and the other Set up rows are hidden. Tap Show hidden Set up routes to see them.

Treble rows left for last

On your last dart from 117 to 129, a Treble whose Single would leave a score with no 2-dart finish (99, 102, 103, etc.) is listed last, in amber. On 122: T20 → 62 / S20 → 102 and T19 → 65 / S19 → 103 are amber, because 102 and 103 can't be finished in two darts.
